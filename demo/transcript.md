# TuonTa video walkthrough

## 00:00 — Welcome to TuonTa

Meet Tuon Ta, an AI study planner built for students. It brings exam planning and document study into one website, helping you turn busy calendars and confusing notes into clear next steps.

## 00:12 — Homepage

The homepage introduces two tools. Choose the study planner to organize upcoming exams, or meet the study buddy to understand your materials. Each tool opens in a separate tab, so you can move between planning and learning.

## 00:26 — Add your exams

In the planner, enter a subject and exam date. Add topics, your confidence level, and the number of hours you want to study. You can also click a future date directly on the calendar to select it.

## 00:39 — Build a manageable routine

Next, choose your daily study budget, from thirty to one hundred twenty minutes, and build your plan. The scheduling algorithm considers deadlines, confidence, and remaining study hours. Sessions stay within your daily budget and finish before each exam.

## 00:55 — Follow your small steps

Your plan lists dates, topics, study methods, and session lengths. Check off completed sessions to track progress, or export the plan as a CSV file. Exam dates and completion progress are saved in this browser, so they remain after a refresh.

## 01:12 — Personalized AI guidance

For extra support, request AI study guidance. It uses your exam information and available time to suggest priorities and techniques. The calendar schedule is controlled by code, while the AI adds explanations and coaching.

## 01:27 — Meet your study buddy

The study buddy also answers general study questions without an upload. Ask for an explanation, a revision strategy, or help understanding a concept. Recent messages provide context for follow-up questions.

## 01:41 — Bring your study material

To study a document, upload a PDF, Word document, text file, or Markdown file, up to ten megabytes. You can also try the built-in biology notes. Text-based PDFs work directly; scanned pages need optical character recognition before uploading.

## 01:59 — Find the key ideas

Choose Summarize for an overview and key points. The response identifies its source and states the coverage used. For longer files, selected sections are analyzed rather than every part of the document.

## 02:12 — Turn reading into revision

Make Notes turns the material into organized revision notes, with concepts, definitions, and important ideas. Responses can include headings and tables to make the information easier to review.

## 02:23 — Ask about your document

You can ask targeted questions about your uploaded material. The assistant uses the document or relevant excerpts to answer, with source labels to help you check the response against the original.

## 02:35 — Test what sticks

Choose Quiz Me to generate five practice questions. Try answering before revealing each suggested answer. This turns passive reading into active recall and helps you notice topics that need another review.

## 02:47 — Keep useful work

Download the conversation to keep your notes and answers, or clear the chat to start fresh. Files and chat stay in the current tab until you leave or clear them. AI responses can be inaccurate, so always compare important claims with your source.

## 03:03 — How the AI works

Tuon Ta uses the open-weight GPT O S S twenty B model through Groq's hosted API. There is no model download for visitors. The API key stays on the server. When you request AI help, selected document text and recent messages are sent to Groq, and usage follows the account's quota.
