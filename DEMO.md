# TuonTa demo guide

Suggested length: 2–3 minutes. Configure the Groq hosting secret and verify the AI connection before recording.

1. Show the homepage and explain: “TuonTa helps students turn exam pressure into manageable study sessions.”
2. Choose **Plan my studies**, opening a separate tab. Click **Try sample exams** or add your own subject and future date by clicking the calendar.
3. Set a daily time budget and click **Build my study plan**. Show that sessions finish before exams and stay within the daily budget. Check off a session, reload to show persistence, and export the CSV.
4. Click **Get AI study guidance** and show the hosted model’s suggestion. Explain the distinction between deterministic scheduling and AI-generated guidance.
5. Open **Study buddy**. Upload a short text PDF or choose the sample notes. Click **Summarize**, ask “What do mitochondria do?”, and show the source excerpt.
6. Choose **Quiz me**. Answer aloud, then reveal the suggested answer. Download the conversation.
7. End with the benefit: a clear next step and more understanding. Explain that GPT-OSS runs through Groq, selected document text is sent to the provider, and usage depends on account quotas. Describe your own contribution and learning.

Suggested screenshots: homepage, calendar with exams and sessions, document Q&A with a source excerpt, quiz with a revealed answer.
